3. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. HEADINGS & TITLES: Space Grotesk
   - Family:       'Space Grotesk', system-ui, sans-serif
   - Fallback:     sans-serif
   - Weights:      600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold)
   - Letterspace:  -0.02em to -0.04em (tight, punchy)
   - Usage:        All section headers (H2, H3, H4), card titles, navigation items, CTA labels
   - Google Font:  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk

B. MONUMENTAL DISPLAY: Unbounded
   - Family:       'Unbounded', 'Space Grotesk', sans-serif
   - Weights:      700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold), 900 (Black)
   - Style:        Normal & Italic
   - Letterspace:  -0.03em to -0.05em
   - Usage:        Main "DAYBREAK" hero headline, "DON'T JUST COUNT DOWN. BUILD SOMETHING."

C. TECHNICAL & METADATA: IBM Plex Mono
   - Family:       'IBM Plex Mono', monospace
   - Weights:      400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
   - Letterspace:  0.02em to 0.08em (widened for mechanical/terminal feel)
   - Usage:        Time codes, schedule slots, coordinates ("BLR 12.9716° N"), pill badges, tags, code snippets
   - Google Font:  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

D. BODY & PROSE: Plus Jakarta Sans
   - Family:       'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif
   - Weights:      400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
   - Line-height:  1.6 to 1.7 (comfortable editorial reading)
   - Usage:        Manifesto philosophy paragraphs, card descriptions, FAQ answers, modal forms
   - Google Font:  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans
